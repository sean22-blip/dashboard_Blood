Outlier Analysis – Blood Orders
The IQR method identified 20 potential outliers (4.8% of 420 records) in the units_issued column, 
with all flagged records containing 7–8 units. Further investigation found that 
emergency orders had the highest proportion of high-volume orders (16.7%). 

However, this category contains only 24 records, so the result should be interpreted cautiously.

These findings describe patterns in the dataset and do not establish that urgency or blood component causes larger orders. 
Since the flagged records do not violate the basic rule that units issued must not exceed units requested,
 they were retained rather than automatically removed.
